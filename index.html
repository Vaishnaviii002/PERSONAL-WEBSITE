PROJECTS

WhatsApp Vault

A product case study: a secure place inside WhatsApp to save the documents people already receive there (Aadhaar, marksheets, offer letters). I diagnosed why Starred Messages fails — it saves references, not copies, so files die as "media unavailable" — and designed a copy-on-save vault with auto-tagging, search, and a biometric lock.

Loom

An AI-powered software delivery platform. I mapped the delivery lifecycle, found that context gets lost at every handoff (request → PRD → dev → review), and designed a role-based flow for five user types with an AI review gate that verifies work against the approved PRD before human sign-off.

Pulse

An AI workflow assistant for Gmail and Calendar. Framed from a real behavior problem — professionals lose focus to constant context-switching — I made the core bet of one unified surface over feature breadth, with AI triage that separates what needs action now from what can wait.
